# Gold Trade Dynamics: Africa, BRICS & G7 (2014–2023) 

## Project Overview
This project analyzes gold export trade flows from South Africa and Côte d’Ivoire to BRICS and G7 nations between 2014 and 2023.

The study evaluates trade dominance, growth trends, geopolitical shifts, and post-COVID recovery patterns using real-world UN Comtrade data.

The objective was to understand how global gold trade power is shifting toward BRICS economies.

---

## Key Objectives
- Compare gold imports: BRICS vs G7
- Analyze South Africa vs Côte d’Ivoire export performance
- Identify post-COVID trade recovery trends
- Calculate year-over-year growth rates
- Perform future trade forecasting (2024–2030)
- Provide strategic trade recommendations

---

## Data Source
- UN Comtrade Database (2014–2023)
- Import data reported by BRICS and G7 nations
- Monthly trade data merged into structured dataset

---

## Key Insights

- South Africa dominated exports, reaching $38.7B in 2023.
- Côte d’Ivoire is a rising exporter with strong growth potential.
- BRICS countries account for a larger and faster-growing share of African gold imports.
- BRICS gold import growth peaked at +816%.
- Post-COVID recovery was significantly stronger in BRICS compared to G7.
- Forecasting indicates continued BRICS dominance in future trade flows.

---

## Strategic Conclusion
Africa’s gold trade is shifting toward BRICS economies, particularly China and India. While G7 nations remain stable partners, BRICS demonstrate faster demand growth and stronger reserve-building strategies.

Long-term trade resilience will depend on infrastructure, diversification, transparency, and geopolitical adaptability.
